InspireWorks IVR Demo

Places an outbound call through Plivo. The caller verifies a 4-digit OTP, picks a language (Level 1), then chooses to hear an audio message or be connected to a live associate (Level 2).

E.164 format: +, country code, then number, e.g. +919876543210.

How to test

  1. Answer the call on the phone.
  2. Enter a wrong 4-digit OTP and hear the re-prompt.
  3. Enter the correct OTP.
  4. Press 1 for English or 2 for Spanish.
  5. Press 1 to hear the audio message, or 2 to be connected to an associate.
